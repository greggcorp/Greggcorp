Hi, I'm Gregg! I am a user experience researcher and writer. This site is where I work through ideas and share what I’m thinking.

I managed research practices at Medium, Hearst Magazines (Runner's World, Elle, Men's Health), Condé Nast (The New Yorker, WIRED, Pitchfork, Bon Appétit), Vox Media (Vox, The Verge, Polygon, Eater), and Mailchimp. I also taught design, branding, and typography at Georgia State University. In a previous life, I created album covers for punk and emo bands, and designed for clients in the music and advertising industries. [Visit the archive](../albums).

I studied journalism at the University of Georgia and earned my MFA in graphic design from the Savannah College of Art & Design, where I focused on user experience research and design. I live with my family in Athens, Georgia.
